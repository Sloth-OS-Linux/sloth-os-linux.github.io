THE PRINCIPLE

If you need the terminal,
that’s my fault.

I’m building Sloth OS on my own. Everyday tasks should be available from the desktop, without asking people to learn command-line tools. If the terminal is required, I still have work to do.

New in Sloth OS 2.0

The biggest update yet.
Still no terminal.

Already running Sloth OS? You get 2.0 as a normal, small update and switch to it at the next restart. New here? Everything below is in the installer.

TV MODE

Your couch, a game console

Steam’s big-screen interface on the whole screen, made for a controller and a TV, with our own start-up video. The games that are not from Steam (Epic, GOG, Amazon, Flatpak and Windows games) show up in it too, with their pictures. Switch to Desktop brings back everything as you left it.

MOVING IN

Your files come along

Sloth OS finds the Windows on the same computer and copies your Desktop, Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos and browser bookmarks, only reading from Windows. New in 2.0: the same from another Linux on the computer, with its apps and Steam games.

⌂OLDER PCS

A second life for old computers

Sloth OS now installs on PCs that start in the old BIOS mode, and a lighter desktop turns itself on where it helps.

⇲ONE CLICK

“I can’t open this file”

A file no app opens yet? Sloth OS offers the right app, installs it with one click, then opens the file.

◈GAMES

One library, game mode

Steam, Heroic, Flatpak and Windows games in one place. While you play, notifications wait and the computer gives the game its best.

✉FEEDBACK

Reports that respect you

Feedback hides your personal details and shows you exactly what is sent, before you send it.

Coming from Windows?

Made for Windows users.
Free to be yours.

Sloth OS is built for people who know Windows and want a computer that is simply theirs: no account to sign in to, no ads in the Start menu, no restart in the middle of your evening. Everything sits where your hands expect it, and you can stop worrying about the rest.

01 / FAMILIAR
⊞

Feels like home on day one

A taskbar and Start menu like Windows 10 or 11, a file manager, right-click menus, and the shortcuts you already use, like Alt+Tab and the Windows key. Prefer the Windows 7 look, a Mac with a Launchpad, Ubuntu, ChromeOS or a floating bar? One click.

02 / YOUR STUFF
⇢

Bring your files and programs

Sloth OS copies your Desktop, Documents, Pictures and browser bookmarks from the Windows (or another Linux) on the same computer, without changing it. Many Windows programs install with a double-click.

03 / SETTINGS
⚙

Settings you’ll recognise

Sloth Center is the Settings app you know: updates, displays, sound, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, backups, and even “Reset this PC”. Offline Help answers the common problems, in your language.

04 / GAMES
◈

Your games come too

Steam, controllers and the graphics drivers are set up for you. “Will it run?” tells you how well a game runs on Sloth OS before you buy it, from thousands of players’ reports.

05 / FREEDOM
✦

Free, in every sense

No licence key, no forced account, no ads, and nothing sent anywhere unless you say yes. No TPM 2.0 needed, so it gives a second life to PCs that Windows 11 turns away. The source is open for anyone to read.

06 / CALM
☾

Your computer, your schedule

Updates download quietly in the background and only what changed. You choose the hour, and quiet hours keep restart reminders away while you play. The previous version is always one click away.

Windows program support

Double-click.
Sloth OS takes it from there.

Double-click an .exe or .msi and Sloth OS tries the settings that work for that program, each program in its own space. Launchers such as Battle.net, EA app and Ubisoft Connect have their own recipes. When a program has a better Linux version (Discord, Chrome, Teams, Epic and GOG through Heroic), Sloth OS offers that first. Not every Windows program runs; when one doesn’t, Sloth OS says so plainly.

    Will it run on my PC?

    Most likely,
    yes.

    Sloth OS runs on most 64-bit PCs, including many that Windows 11 turns away. Try it from the USB stick first: nothing on your computer changes until you choose to install.
